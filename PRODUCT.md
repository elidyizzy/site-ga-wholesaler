# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

B2B buyers across six segments who want to launch or scale a private-label food/beverage brand without building production infrastructure themselves: cafés and restaurants, specialty grocers/delis, distributors and wholesalers, supermarket chains, corporate offices (coffee-service programs), and individual entrepreneurs/creators. Confirmed with the client: no single segment is prioritized as lead persona today — messaging must work horizontally across all six.

## Product Purpose

GA Food Wholesaler helps a business or entrepreneur that already has a brand idea turn it into a real, sellable private-label product — from diagnosis through production and launch — without the client having to assemble that infrastructure themselves.

## Positioning

GA Food is not a roastery or a single factory-for-hire; it positions itself as an integrated private-label brand-development hub ("Private Label 360°") spanning diagnosis, product development, branding/design, packaging, regulatory/registration, production/logistics, and launch/support, all under one coordinated team. Coffee is a strong vertical but explicitly not the whole business — a neighboring roaster or co-packer could not truthfully claim the same end-to-end structure.

## Operating Context

Client engagement starts with a diagnostic conversation (WhatsApp/e-mail/qualifying form on the future Contato page) and proceeds through the seven Private Label 360° stages. GA Food's own facilities (production floor, tasting room, design studio, showroom/office) are the operating environment shown in photography — never the client's own kitchen or retail floor, and never a literal farm/crop field (GA Food is not a rural producer).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seven-stage methodology is a confirmed, real service structure, not page filler: Diagnóstico & Estratégia, Desenvolvimento de Produto, Branding & Design, Embalagem, Registros & Regulatório, Produção & Logística, Lançamento & Suporte.
- Stages are modular — confirmed with the client 2026-09-12, resolving a contradiction in the briefing deck (page 2 "serviços independentes ou projeto completo" vs. page 3 "um projeto, um parceiro responsável pela jornada"). A prospect can hire a single stage (e.g. only Embalagem) or the full Private Label 360° journey; the site must not imply the 7 stages are an all-or-nothing bundle.
- Café Private Label is a distinct, highlighted vertical among the services, not the company's core identity.
- No e-commerce/checkout — this is a lead-generation/institutional site (Persuade mode) that qualifies leads through a future contact/diagnostic form, WhatsApp, and e-mail.
- Site is being built page by page; only the Home exists today. Planned pages (Private Label 360° detail, Serviços, Café Private Label, Para Quem É, Como Funciona, Por Que GA Food, Cases/Projetos, Contato/Diagnóstico) do not exist yet — the Home should not link to routes that don't exist, and should use in-page anchors until those pages ship.

## Product Principles

1. Structure over hype — the story is the integrated 7-stage system, not superlatives ("somos os melhores" is explicitly banned in the briefing).
2. Process before product — photography and copy dramatize how the brand gets built, not just showcase finished packaging.
3. One saturated moment — the logo is the only place color intensity happens; everything else stays warm and restrained so the logo and the product photography can carry visual weight.
4. Coffee is a vertical, not the identity — never let café imagery or copy dominate to the point the company reads as "just a roastery."
5. Earn trust without borrowed proof — since no testimonials or case studies exist, credibility comes from the clarity and craft of the method itself, never from fabricated numbers or claims.
